About the Team

How We Operate

Team members will be selected based on merit and performance. The US Army Drone Team is not currently a centralized unit (TDA assignment). All Soldiers accepted on the team will remain with their unit. All Training and competition will be funded as Temporary Duty (TDY) by the 1st Aviation Brigade at Ft Rucker, AL.

Expect training and competitions to occur quarterly at a minimum. We will reevaluate a Soldier's position as a primary on the team if the Soldier consistently is unable to attend training and/or competitions quarterly. Training and competition will be both within the Continental United States (CONUS) and Outside the Continental United States (OCONUS). Soldiers on the team will be expected to acquire an Official Passport.

Only Army Service Members are permitted to apply and join the US Army Drone Team. If you have questions on expectations while on the US Army Drone Team please reach out to usarmydroneteam@army.mil.

Future Goals

Within the next year we hope to establish the US Army Drone Team as an Army Sports team similar to the Army Rugby Team.
