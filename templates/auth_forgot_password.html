Reset your password

To keep the US Army Drone Team database secure, password resets are handled manually by the site administrators. If you have forgotten your password, please follow the steps below and our team will assist you as soon as possible.

  • Email army.droneteam@army.mil from your registered account.
  • Include your full name, username, and unit or organization.
  • If you no longer have access to the registered email, provide a supervisor or sponsor who can confirm your identity.

Once your request is received, an administrator will verify your details and send you instructions for setting a new password.
